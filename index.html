    Getting started Prontera

    1. Claim your Freebies. Talk to the Kafra Employee (prontera 145,197) and pick Claim Freebies. You get 3 days of VIP, two random Poring costumes, 5 MVP Soul, 6 Light White Potion boxes, 6 Blue Potion boxes, 3 Battle Manuals and 3 JOB Battle Manuals. Freebies can be claimed once per computer.
    2. Change job at the Job Master (prontera 164,195). Rebirth and Baby classes are available, and Platinum skills are given automatically when you change job.
    3. Start your Daily Quest with @dailyquest.
    4. Turn on Autohunt with @ah when you want to farm. You have 3 free hours every day.
    5. Check your Weapon Mastery with @wi. It levels up as you hunt.
    Tip: Legacia Support (prontera 153,193, and one in most towns) heals and buffs you for free. The Warper stands in every town.

    Autohunt @ah

    Autohunt is Legacia's built-in, server-side auto attack. Your character explores the map, fights monsters with the skills you choose, drinks potions, teleports when the area is empty and loots for you. Third-party bots are not allowed. Use this instead.

    Time

    • Everyone gets 3 hours free every day, reset at midnight server time.
    • Free time is shared by your account and your computer, so extra accounts on the same PC don't get extra free time.
    • Buy more with Autohunt Time tickets in the Cash Shop (Consumables tab): 1 hour for 17 CP, 5 hours for 85 CP, 24 hours for 408 CP. Bought time never expires.

    Rewards while autohunting

    Autohunting gives 50% EXP, 50% drop rate and 50% Weapon Mastery EXP compared with playing yourself. @ah and @wi show the current values.

    Settings in the @ah menu

    OptionWhat it does

    Attack skillsUp to 3 skills, tried in order. If none can be used, you attack normally.
    Buff skillsUp to 3 self buffs, recast whenever they run out.

    Auto teleportTeleports after 1 to 60 seconds without a monster. Choose Teleport skill, Infinite Fly Wing, Fly Wing, or Auto (tries them in that order).
    Crowd teleportTeleports away when too many monsters (2 to 30) are within 3 cells of you.

    Limits: up to 2 characters per computer can autohunt at once, and only 1 of them offline. Autohunt avoids warp portals and doesn't steal monsters other players are fighting. Autohunting characters show an [AutoHunt] tag and a blue aura.

    Weapon Mastery @wi

    Every weapon type belongs to one of four masteries. The weapon in your right hand decides which mastery is active and which one gains EXP. Left-hand weapons don't count. Masteries go up to level 128, and each level makes its bonuses stronger.

    STR

    Onslaught

    1H/2H Sword, 1H/2H Spear, 1H/2H Axe, Mace, Knuckle. Damage vs all monsters, Base ATK, critical damage, ASPD, and splash attack at Lv 128.

    AGI

    Swiftness

    Dagger, Katar, Fuuma Shuriken, Book. ASPD, move speed, Flee, Perfect Dodge, Double Attack, crit, and HP drain at Lv 128.

    DEX

    Ranged

    Bow, Instrument, Whip, Guns. Damage vs all monsters, long range damage, flat ASPD, Hit, and +2 attack range at Lv 128.

    INT

    Mystical

    Rod, 2H Staff, plus some magic daggers and swords (e.g. Elemental Sword). Magic damage, MATK, cast time, SP, healing power, and no cast cancel at Lv 128.

    • How to level: hunt with the weapon equipped. Mastery EXP comes from the Job EXP of your kills, and higher level weapons give more.
    • Party: when a party member kills a monster, the rest of the party on the map gets 50% of that mastery EXP.
    • Mastery bonuses scale with your base stat (STR/AGI/DEX/INT without gear).
    • The Weapon Mastery Guide (prontera 147,170) lists every bonus in game.

    Party play and MVP drop share

    When anyone in your party kills an MVP, every other member on the same map rolls the MVP's whole drop table for themselves, with the same chances as the killer. A 0.1% card is a 0.1% chance for each of you. The killer keeps their normal drops.

    • Shared drops arrive unidentified in your inventory. Rare ones (1% or lower) are announced to the whole server.
    • This also works for quest and instance MVPs such as Ktullanux and Thanatos Phantom.
    • Alts on the same computer don't get a roll. Bring real friends.
    • Weapon Mastery EXP is shared at 50% too (see Weapon Mastery).

    World Boss prontera 136,171

    Every monster anyone kills adds 1 soul to the server's soul count. At 15,000 souls, any level 99 player can summon the World Boss at the World Boss NPC. It appears on a random field (Juno, Einbroch, Lighthalzen and more) with guardians and stays for 1 hour.

    • Everyone who deals any damage to the boss gets rewards by mail.
    • Always: 17 to 25 MVP Soul. Top damage dealer: 10 to 15 extra MVP Soul.
    • Chance drops: Fallen Angel Wing (1%), Bloody Branch, Enriched Elunium/Oridecon (5%), and 10% each for Kafra Card, LV10 Blessing/Agil Scrolls, Acid Bomb box, Poison Bottles, Yggdrasil Berry Box, Infinite Fly Wing and VIP 1 Day.
    • Only one character per computer on the boss map.

    War of Emperium WOE Information, prontera 149,193

    Schedule: every Wednesday, 20:00 to 21:00 server time. The castle is Himinn (schg_cas01). Pick WoE Castle [Himinn] in the Warper to get there.
    • The guild that holds the castle when WoE ends gets the castle reward.
    • WoE contributor reward: anyone who really took part can claim a reward at the WOE Information NPC for up to 6 days after WoE, even if they were offline when it ended. You need 15 minutes of active time on the castle map (AFK time doesn't count) and at least one of: a player kill, 5,000 damage, 5,000 healing on guild/alliance members, or 10 support skills on them.
    • Contributor reward: 10,000,000z, 20 Acid Bomb 10 Box, 5 Poison Bottle 5 Box, 2 Cash Point[10], 10 Light White Potion Box, 10 Blue Potion Box. One claim per computer each WoE.
    • Rankings: @woerank shows the player ranking (rating goes up and down with kills and deaths). The WOE Information NPC also shows guild stats for the last WoE and all time, and your own stats.

    PvP

    PvP kills are tracked on the Ladder (prontera 166,198) and with @pvpladder. The PvP-StatsViewer (prontera 147,163) shows your own PvP stats. Getting a kill (or dying) on a PvP map also completes the PvP task of the Daily Quest.

    Quest Shop (headgear crafting) prontera 147,166

    The Quest Shop crafts classic headgears from monster loot. Pick a quest page (Quest I to VIII), choose a headgear, and it shows the items you need. You can preview the headgear before you make it. Every Quest Shop headgear also appears in the Daily Point Shop rotation.

    Cash Shop and Cash Points

    Open the Cash Shop from the button in your game window. It sells VIP, Autohunt Time tickets, convenience boxes (stat dishes, converters, weight boosts), and costume and headgear items. Cash Points (CP) come from donations and from in-game sources:

    • WoE contributor reward: 2 Cash Point[10] each WoE.
    • Event rewards and GM promo codes (claim them at the Kafra, Promotional Code).

    Cash Point items (10 / 100 / 1000 / 10000) turn into CP when you use them.

    Events

    7 Nights of Jack until Nov 15, 2026

    Talk to Jack the Pumpkin Keeper (prontera 159,183) or type @halloween. Clear one night per day (resets at 00:00) by hunting monsters, finding Pumpkin Ghosts in Payon, Geffen, Alberta and Morocc, and bringing items. Each night gives Pumpkin Candy, EXP and items. Spend candy in Jack's Candy Shop.

    After all 7 nights you get a random Halloween headgear costume, a random costume garment, 5 Cash Point[10], 10 Elite Siege Supply Box, 10 Token of Siegfried and 100 MVP Soul. Missing a day doesn't reset anything. Progress is per account, and only one account per computer can do the event.

    Regular events

    • Weekend Double EXP every Friday 00:00 to Monday 00:00.
    • Devil Square every 2 hours.
    • Cluckers: a GM-run chicken hunt in Prontera with a prize.

    Fair play rules

    Read this: Legacia uses Gepard Shield. Third-party bots are not allowed. Use @ah instead.
    • The client allows 2 logins per computer.
    • Many rewards are limited per computer, not just per account: Freebies, Daily Quest, the Halloween event, promo codes, autohunt free time, MVP drop share and the WoE contributor reward.
    • One character per computer on MVP maps and on the World Boss map.
    • Costumes from the Gacha Machine and from monsters can't be traded.
    • Don't feel pressured to donate. Legacia is free to play.
