    Episode 1: The Hollow Crown Preview

    Legacia's own story. A thousand years ago the kingdom of Legacia stood where Prontera is now, until King Aldric tried to make it eternal and tore it out of time. The Chronicle Stones that seal it are cracking, and rifts open into echoes of the lost kingdom.

    Base Lv 99Start at Archivist Sera, Prontera Castle (prt_castle 125,51)
    2 chaptersGlast Heim hunt, then a rift into an echo of Glast Heim
    Legacy ShardsTrade them for Legacia weapons and headgears
    10~20 shardsEvery day for clearing the rift again
    Read more: chapters, rewards and the Shard Exchange

    Chapters

    • Chapter 1, The Stone That Remembers. Defeat 100 monsters in any Glast Heim map and bring 100 Skel-Bone and 10 Cursed Ruby. Reward: Echo Lens, Legacy Emblem Lv1, 10 Legacy Shards.
    • Chapter 2, Knights Without a King. Sera opens a rift into an echo of old Glast Heim, solo or with your party (the leader opens it, 2 hours). Ser Corvin leads you through the great hall (3 waves), the west chapel, the east forge and the north barracks, then up to the throne hall to face Oathbreaker Valen. The echoes are tough: bring a party. Reward: 30 Legacy Shards.
    • After the story the rift stays open: you can clear it once a day for 10~20 Legacy Shards (resets 04:00, see your quest window). You must be inside when Valen falls.

    Weapon Mastery @wi

    Hunt with a weapon and its mastery levels up, up to Lv 128. Only the right-hand weapon counts.

    STR

    Onslaught

    Swords, spears, axes, maces, knuckles. Damage vs all, Base ATK, crit damage, splash at Lv 128.

    AGI

    Swiftness

    Daggers, katars, Fuuma, books. ASPD, move speed, Flee, Double Attack, HP drain at Lv 128.

    DEX

    Ranged

    Bows, instruments, whips, guns. Damage vs all, ranged damage, ASPD, +2 range at Lv 128.

    INT

    Mystical

    Rods, staves, some magic daggers/swords. MATK, magic damage, cast time, SP, no cast cancel at Lv 128.

    Read more: how leveling works
    • Mastery EXP comes from the Job EXP of your kills. Higher level weapons give more.
    • Party members on the map get 50% of your mastery EXP.
    • Bonuses scale with your base stat (STR/AGI/DEX/INT without gear).
    • The Weapon Mastery Guide (prontera 147,170) lists every bonus.
